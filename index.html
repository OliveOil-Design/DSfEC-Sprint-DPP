Care and repair

Wipe panels clean, avoid pressure washing. Cracked tiles can be re-pressed, not binned.

Full care & repair instructions

Everyday care

  • Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap; avoid solvents and pressure washing.
  • Keep out of prolonged direct heat — HDPE can soften above ~60°C.
  • Refresh compost in the planter pockets each spring.

If a tile is cracked or damaged

  • Small cracks: tiles can be gently re-pressed at a workshop — they do not need binning.
  • Beyond repair: return it (below) so the material re-enters the local remake cycle.
